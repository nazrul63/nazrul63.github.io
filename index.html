I am a Ph.D. student in Information Science at the Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST), Japan, in the Computational Systems Biology Laboratory, supported by the JST GRANITE Program scholarship. My doctoral research uses machine learning to predict the mechanisms of action (MOA) of antibiotic compounds, with the wider aim of supporting work against drug-resistant bacteria.

Before my Ph.D., I spent about five years in the data domain, most of it as Database Administrator and Team Lead at Biman Bangladesh Airlines, where I built dashboards, reporting tools and ETL pipelines on airline operations data and led digital transformation projects.

At NAIST my work spans cheminformatics, medical imaging, drug response prediction and LLM-based knowledge extraction, with a consistent focus on leakage-aware evaluation and on whether models hold up on data they have not seen.

I work mainly in Python (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, TensorFlow) and SQL, and also bring web development experience with Laravel, JavaScript and Bootstrap.

A Three-Stage Hierarchical Pipeline for Mechanism of Action Annotation of Antibiotic Compounds

A S M Nazrul Islam, Muhammad Imran Khan, Ahmad Kamal Nasution, Naoaki Ono, Shigehiko Kanaya, Md. Altaf-Ul-Amin

2026 IEEE International Conference on Future Machine Learning and Data Science (FMLDS 2026), Kobe, Japan.

A Multi-Label Benchmark for Retinal Disease Classification on BRSET: Strong Baselines and the Limits of Label-Correlation Graphs

Muhammad Imran Khan, A S M Nazrul Islam, Shigehiko Kanaya, Md. Altaf-Ul-Amin

2026 IEEE International Conference on Future Machine Learning and Data Science (FMLDS 2026), Kobe, Japan.

First multi-label benchmark on the Brazilian BRSET fundus dataset across eleven classes, with patient-level splits; a class-weighted baseline (macro-AUC 0.913) outperforms the ML-GCN label-correlation graph at every edge threshold tested.

An EGFR-Pathway-Aware Multi-Omics Ensemble for Afatinib Drug Response Prediction in Cancer Cell Lines

Muhammad Imran Khan, Taran Khan, A S M Nazrul Islam, Md. Altaf-Ul-Amin

2026 IEEE International Conference on Future Machine Learning and Data Science (FMLDS 2026), Kobe, Japan.

Leakage-controlled, pathway-informed ensemble of XGBoost and graph neural networks (GCN, GAT, GraphSAGE) on 510 CCLE/GDSC cell lines, reaching the best Spearman correlation (0.496) and R² (0.330) among the models compared.

LLM-Assisted Knowledge Graph Enrichment from GitHub Repositories: A Preliminary Empirical Study with Cross-Vendor LLM Comparison

Muhammad Imran Khan, A S M Nazrul Islam, Adnan Imranul Islam, Rafia Sultana, Md. Rajibul Alam

4th International Conference on Computing Advancements (ICCA 2026), ACM, Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Constrained-schema extraction of eight relation types from 30 ML repositories, evaluated against a 922-triple hand-annotated gold standard; two LLMs from different vendors both far outperform a regex baseline and show complementary strengths.

Beyond Headline Accuracy: A Leakage-Aware Re-Evaluation of Deep Learning for Glaucoma Detection Across Datasets

Muhammad Imran Khan, A S M Nazrul Islam, Mohiuddin Saifullah

IEEE 3rd International Conference on Computing, Applications and Systems (COMPAS 2026), University of Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Shows that a high-scoring glaucoma classifier is inflated by dataset-source confounding (pooled AUROC 0.979 vs. within-source 0.886) and degrades sharply on three external datasets, and recommends reporting within-source, cross-dataset, calibration and subgroup metrics.
